Conferences & Competitions

Expand your network, build leadership skills, and test your engineering abilities through student events.

Engineering Conferences

Annual conferences attended by ESSS.

Executives Meeting (EM)

Hosted by WESST • May

Helps transition incoming executive teams and launches the WESST General Assembly with representatives from western Canadian schools.

President's Meeting (PM)

Hosted by CFES • September

Brings together student engineering leaders from across Canada for the first General Assembly of the Canadian Federation of Engineering Students.

Annual General Meeting Retreat (AGMR)

Hosted by WESST • October

Features workshops on student society involvement alongside the second WESST General Assembly for selected delegates.

Conference on Diversity in Engineering (CDE)

Hosted by CFES • November

Explores strategies and initiatives for more inclusivity and diverse environments within the engineering profession.

Canadian Engineering Leadership Conference (CELC)

Hosted by CFES • January

Based on leadership and how to be more involved as a leader in respective engineering societies.

Conference on Sustainability in Engineering (CSE)

Hosted by CFES • March

Based on sustainability and being more environmentally friendly in engineering.

Engineering Competitions

Gain hands-on experience and demonstrate your skills in competitive challenges.

SFU Engineering Competition

Organized by the ESSS, MSESS, and SEESS with categories designed for all undergraduate skill levels.

Western Engineering Competition

Brings together winning teams from western Canadian universities who advance past the local competitions.

Canadian Engineering Competition

Features the top champions from regional engineering competitions across the country.
