Our Mission

The Engineering Science Student Society (ESSS) is a Department Student Union dedicated to supporting SFU engineering students academically, personally, and professionally. We enhance student life through events, educational resources, and community initiatives, while mediating between students and university administration. We also represent SFU nationally as partners with the CFES and WESST, advocating for student interests and engineering accreditation.
